About

COVADONGA ALPERI operates as an Asturias-based Independent Creative Studio focusing on visual identity, editorial design, art direction, and tactile branding experiences.

Led by designer and art director Covadonga Alperi, the studio moves fluidly between meticulous technical execution and deep conceptual narratives, crafting bespoke visual languages with high emotional resonance.

Whether developing a brand's core identity from the ground up, curating product aesthetics, or directing physical and digital editorial pieces, our work remains intentional, tactile, and rooted in the aesthetics of now.

Services

Creative Direction

We act as a strategic and visual partner, shaping the conceptual, artistic, and narrative foundation of brands, collections, and campaigns from the ground up.

Art Direction & Product Photography

Curating set design, still life compositions, and aesthetic visual narratives tailored for fashion labels, jewelry, design objects, and lifestyle products.

Brand Identity & Strategy

Crafting comprehensive visual systems, distinct typographic hierarchies, color palettes, and brand guidelines that give independent brands an authentic and memorable presence.

Packaging & Tactile Experience

Designing physical packaging, unboxing experiences, material exploration, and production specifications with a focus on tactile detailing and fine finishes.

Editorial & Print Design

High-end publications, lookbooks, exhibition graphics, art books, and brand collateral that celebrate the craftsmanship of print culture and typography.

Digital & Interactive Presence

Translating tactile studio sensibilities into modern web experiences, portfolio architectures, and curated digital media assets.
